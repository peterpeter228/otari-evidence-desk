# Local participant recording checklist

Purpose: an original 3–5 minute screen recording with the participant's own voice. This is a rehearsal checklist, not a generated voice/video or a submission. Do not record credentials, account screens, private configuration or the terminal that enrolled the key.

## Before recording

- Use the reviewed local demo launch instructions. Keep the app loopback-only; do not publish or expose its port.
- Check the current result's mode, original timestamp, run ID, model identities, request IDs, costs and conflict evidence.
- A saved live result is **replay**, not a new request. Keep its replay badge visible. Fixture mode is deterministic, not AI.
- Only use the built-in synthetic Atlas/Boreal materials for this bounded live demo. They are invented examples, not real product claims.
- Do not repeat a paid action to improve the recording. A pending or uncertain operation requires reconciliation, not another click or restart.
- Hide unrelated tabs and notifications. Test microphone and screen readability; use your own voice, not AI-generated narration or impersonation.

## Suggested sequence

1. **0:00–0:35 — Problem.** Show why a source-backed statement and agreement between sources are different. Explain the comparison question and synthetic source material.
2. **0:35–1:15 — Honest mode.** Point to the fixture/replay/fresh-live label. For replay, say the displayed result came from a prior real Otari run and this screen is not sending new calls. For a fresh authorized operation, show one deliberate start and its status; do not refresh to trigger a retry.
3. **1:15–2:15 — Useful result.** Show the two roles: Qwen extracts cited statements; GPT OSS reviews the original sources. Open the separate conflict section and the exact 30-day and 14-day quotes. A faithful quotation may remain source-supported while the policy conflict stays unresolved.
4. **2:15–3:00 — Evidence and limits.** Show provider/model/request IDs and the run's reported USD cost, distinguishing it from cumulative test spend. Explain that exact quotation matching does not prove source truth or general semantic accuracy.
5. **3:00–3:40 — Practical output.** Show a result export and error handling if available in this mode. Keep fixture-only controls clearly separate from the live demonstration.
6. **3:40–4:30 — Integration feedback.** Briefly explain the real issues encountered: client timeout followed by delayed server success, precise settlement recovery, structured-output limits, and the separation of citation support from conflicts. Say what was fixed and what remains unverified.

## Do not claim

- Guaranteed accuracy, safety, profitability, cash prizes or winning.
- Native routing/fallback, tools, guardrails or per-user isolation merely because a catalog lists them or the app has local safeguards.
- Production readiness, public judge access, deployment or submission unless separately completed and verified.
- That every historical test succeeded. The retained report includes failures and one coached fixture test that is excluded from independent semantic evidence.

## Before final submission

Check the latest organizer requirements and deadline, public repository/judge access authorization, contest branch and first-integration commit, participant-owned demo, honest feedback, and submission receipt. Recording this demo does not submit the project.
